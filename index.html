How it works

Enzymes are cleared from the blood at a roughly constant fraction per hour, so each level halves every half-life. Going back in time reverses that: the value h hours earlier is today’s value × 2h / half-life.

AST clears fastest (about 17 hours), so it climbs fastest as you go back. ALT clears slowly (about 72 hours). The estimates assume clearance alone, with no ongoing injury, which is why they are most useful after a single, time-limited insult such as ischemia or a toxic ingestion.

  • Enter when the labs were drawn and any of AST, ALT, and LDH.
  • Adjust the half-lives, the number of steps, or the step size under Assumptions.
  • The table lists each step with its date and time; Copy table copies it as text.
